# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Welcome different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code and observable behavior.
- Protect private information and security reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and community safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information, sustained disruption and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method in the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are reviewed confidentially and in good faith. The maintainer may edit, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
