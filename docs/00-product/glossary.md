# Product Glossary

## Expedition

One playable session from station initialization to completion or voluntary termination.

## Cycle

The deterministic unit in which priorities, crew needs, tasks, resource production and event triggers are evaluated.

## Station

The player's current platform, modules, resources, crew and expedition state.

## Module

A station construction with an operational state, resource effect and possible repair requirement.

## Crew member

One of three people assigned to the station. A crew member has a role, trait, needs, relationships, current task and bounded recent memories.

## Priority

The player's global operational preference: survival, power, research or exploration.

## Event

A deterministic trigger with structured context, two or three player choices and engine-calculated consequences.

## Narrative

Presentation text generated by a validated AI response or deterministic fallback. Narrative never determines game state.

## Memory

A short, bounded crew recollection derived from an event. Memories provide future narrative context and do not create new rules.

## Resume token

An opaque secret stored locally to recover an anonymous server-side expedition. It is not a user account or game-state copy.
